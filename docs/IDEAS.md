# Ideas for later

Things Rusty could do next. Not built yet.

## Helpful

- **Push-to-talk:** hold a key (or click Rusty) and talk; he transcribes it locally and sends it to
  the active Claude session as a prompt.
- **Test reactions:** when Claude runs tests (`PostToolUse` for `Bash` with a test command), a fist
  pump on green and a facepalm on red.
- **Break reminder:** after about 90 minutes of steady work, a stretch and "time for a short break?".
- **Context meter:** a small bar on his screen showing how full the session's context is.
- **Several sessions waiting:** a badge with the number of sessions that need your OK; click to hear which.
- **Commit and PR celebrations:** a little jump when Claude commits, a bigger one when a PR is opened.

## Skills

- **Rubber duck:** you explain a bug out loud; Claude only asks short questions until you spot it.
- **Story time / explain like Rusty:** short spoken explanations of a concept, with an example.
- **Standup:** a 30-second spoken "yesterday, today, blockers" from the event log and git.
